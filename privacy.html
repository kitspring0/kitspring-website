Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains, in general terms, how KitSpring handles information when you visit this website or contact us. This is a starter policy and should be reviewed for your actual operations and applicable legal requirements before launch.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details and message. The contact form on this website prepares an email in your own email application; it does not send form data to a KitSpring server.

Website information

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the website. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded services or additional forms are added later, this policy should be updated before they are activated.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, provide support and manage business communications. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Links to external services, including WhatsApp and any future checkout provider, are governed by those providers' own privacy terms. Review their policies before sharing information or making a purchase.

Retention and security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to communications and retain them only as needed for legitimate business or legal purposes. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and contact

For a privacy-related enquiry or request, email kitspring@gmail.com.

Updates

We may update this policy as the website and services develop. The date above will be revised when changes are made.
