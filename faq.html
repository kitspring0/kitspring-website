Frequently asked questions.

Useful answers about KitSpring, our catalogue and how digital resources will work.

What is KitSpring?

KitSpring is an independent digital-products brand creating practical resources for work and everyday life.

What kinds of products will you offer?

The catalogue is planned to include spreadsheets, templates, workbooks, PDFs, guides and toolkits across five collections.

Can I purchase every item shown?

Not yet. Items labelled Coming Soon are in development and cannot currently be purchased. A purchase link will appear only when a product is genuinely available.

How will I receive a digital product?

Delivery details will be shown on the product page and at the external checkout when a product is released.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment methods will depend on the checkout provider configured for each released product. We will publish accurate details before accepting orders.

Can I request a custom resource?

You can contact us with your idea. We cannot guarantee that every request will become a product.

How can I contact KitSpring?

Email kitspring@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +234 912 873 2396.
