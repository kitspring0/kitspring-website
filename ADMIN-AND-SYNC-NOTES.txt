KITSPRING — UPDATED UPLOADED FILES

The 10 files in this folder have been synchronized to the corresponding versions
inside KitSpring-Complete-Website.zip.

Important:
- The public homepage is index.html.
- The administrator sign-in page is admin/index.html.
- On the deployed site, open /admin/ to reach the administrator sign-in page.
- The public homepage intentionally does not contain the administrator login form.
- Keep admin/index.html, admin/admin.js, admin/admin.css and admin/config.js together
  in the full website project for the admin area to work.
- Configure the Supabase public project URL and anon/publishable key in the
  appropriate config.js files before connecting the site. Never put a service_role
  or secret key in browser-side files.
- The sitemap and robots.txt still contain the example domain from the ZIP build;
  replace kitspring.example with your real domain before launch.
